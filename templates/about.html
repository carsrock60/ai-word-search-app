About WordGen

WordGen helps students, teachers, and puzzle enthusiasts create printable word searches in seconds.

✨ 100% Free & Fast
🎯 High Quality Themes
✍️ Manual Word Input
📄 Professional PDF Export
🔑 Answer Keys Included

💡 Why I Built This

Have you ever been surfing the web for half an hour, and finding the same, boring word searches? This was exactly what happened to 9-year-old me. Instead of searching the internet, I made this completely free application for everyone to use. This tool does all the work for you, so you don't have to spend a long time surfing the internet. I hope you enjoy!
