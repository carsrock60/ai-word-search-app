Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WordGen.

What is WordGen?

WordGen is an AI-powered word search generator that can create puzzles from almost any topic in seconds.

Is WordGen Free?

Yes! WordGen is completely free to use.

How do I make a word search?

Simply enter a topic and click Generate Puzzle. Within seconds, WordGen creates a brand-new word search based on your chosen topic.

Can I create a custom word search?

Absolutely! You can create custom word searches by entering your own words, separated by commas. WordGen will generate a puzzle using the exact words you provide.

How do teachers make printable word searches?

Teachers can create printable word searches for free using WordGen. Enter a topic, choose one of our themes, or provide your own word list. Then generate the puzzle, download the PDF, and print it for your students.

Can I generate a word search from a topic?

Yes! Simply enter any topic and WordGen will generate a relevant word list automatically. Whether it's food, Italy, basketball, or the history of the steam engine, WordGen can create a puzzle in seconds.

Why should I use WordGen instead of other generators?

Unlike traditional word search generators that require you to enter every word manually, WordGen automatically generates relevant words from your chosen topic. You can create puzzles instantly, solve them online, and download printable PDFs.

Can I use WordGen on my phone?

Absolutely! WordGen works on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. You can even install WordGen as an app directly from your browser for quick access.

Can I solve word searches online?

Yes! WordGen includes an interactive puzzle solver that lets you find and highlight words directly in your browser.

Are the puzzles different every time?

Yes. Word placement is randomized, making every generated puzzle unique.

What topics work best?

WordGen can generate puzzles for almost any topic, including sports, science, geography, history, programming, animals, and more.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. You can start generating puzzles immediately.
