    About WordGen

    WordGen helps students, teachers, and puzzle enthusiasts create printable word searches in seconds.

    Why I Built This

    WordGen started as a personal project to make creating educational word searches faster and easier. Instead of manually typing out grids and hiding words, this AI-powered tool does all the heavy lifting instantly. It's designed to look beautiful on screen and print perfectly on paper.
